## College-Level Service
At the college level, I served on the Faculty Council from 2022-2023, leaving after accepting the Associate Department Chair role in February 2023. During my short tenure on Faculty Council, I assisted Alex Rister in initiating a Faculty Council Collegiality Award to address faculty concerns on receiving a collegiality award from administration. My short time on the Faculty Council was praised by its chair **[Faculty Council Member Evaluation]**. I also volunteered to lead the Fall 2025 efforts to revise the Faculty Council charter after faculty voted to temporarily suspend the council. These efforts led to a successful restart of Faculty Council in January 2026. 

I created and maintained a Faculty Activity Reporting system on behalf of the college. This system allowed for ease of faculty submitting their activity to a single location and automated, formatted reporting of faculty activity for efficient monthly and bi-yearly reporting. I met with representatives of both College of Aviation (Kimberly Luthi) and College of Business (Maria Petrescu) to discuss how they could implement a similar system. Maria thought the set up was excellent, stating in private email correspondence: 

> I love this! Your survey is so much better than the Forms we've been using so far. If you don't mind exporting the survey so I can import it into my Qualtrics, as well as your Python script, that would be great. Our senior associate dean who's been handling this left, and I'm trying to catch up on this and make it more efficient. 

Maria was involved in creating the new Watermark activity reporting and confirmed it was partially modeled after the Faculty Reporting system I shared with her. 

I also served as an inter-department member of the 2024 hiring committee for a tenured Department Chair for the Department of Human Factors and Social Science. 

## Department-Level Service
At the department level, I have sustained high-level leadership since accepting the Associate Department Chair role in February 2023. As Associate Department Chair, I performed all tasks expected of me as well as used my expertise in data science to improve admin efficiencies. For example, course monitors in the department asked for lists of instructors teaching courses they were monitors for by term and I created programming scripts to create such lists from raw Campus Solutions reports. In addition, course monitors asked for a yearly accumulation of end-of-course evaluations for courses they monitored, which I created programming scripts to provide. 

After the department chair hiring failed in January 2024, I accepted being Acting Department Chair (while still being Associate Department Chair) until another hiring cycle could be completed successfully. I stayed on as Associate Department Chair for another year to assist the newly hired chair, Tim Smith, and passed on the role in order to start as the Program Chair for the new Bachelor in Data Science in August 2025. The first year of the program saw major success and had 54 declared students by August 2026. During these years of administrative service I continually shifted the courses I was mentor for as the Department of Mathematics, Science, and Technology had heavy faculty turnover. I chaired one tenure-track candidate search in Physical Science in 2024-2025 while participating as a member in three other candidate searches: tenured candidate in Computer/Data Science in 2026, tenured department chair in 2024 **[Higgins Search Letter]**, and tenure-track canidate in Data Science in 2022-2023.
