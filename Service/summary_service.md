# ERAU Service
A full list of the varied service activity I've performed over the past five years can be found in the automated Watermark **[Service]** report. This letter synthesizes that activity to illustrate sustained and progressive levels of service among 3 threads: college and university service, departmental service, and professional society service. 

## College and University Service
Given my extensive college and departmental service at the University of Florida **[CV, pg 12]**, I immediately sought out impactful service opportunities at ERAU. In my first year I was elected the ERAU-WW Faculty Senate Academic Technology committee. With my expertise in academic technology, I was voted chair of the committee for all three years. Primary duties for the committee included systematic review of various educational technologies such as YellowDig. This trans-college service allowed me to build collegial relationships with faculty outside of my college such as with Brent Terwilliger (COA) who I eventually submitted an NSF grant with. It also allowed me to directly interact with university initiatives as an expert in educational technology, such as participating in the hiring of a Director of IT Service Management & Worldwide IT Service as well as the ERAU-WW End-of-Course Faculty Survey committee to focus student evaluation questions. My work on the council was praised by the Vice Speaker of the Worldwide Faculty Senate **[FS Committee Chair Evaluation]**. I then waited for another committee position to open related to my expertise, which occurred in October 2026 when I was elected to the ERAU-WW Faculty Senate Undergraduate Curriculum & Degrees committee. Throughout this time I also served as a university reviewer for internal grants and was frequently called upon to review mathematics and engineering-adjacent research grants. 

At the college level, I served on the Faculty Council from 2022-2023, leaving after accepting the Associate Department Chair role in February 2023. During my short tenure on Faculty Council, I assisted Alex Rister in initiating a Faculty Council Collegiality Award to address faculty concerns on receiving a collegiality award from administration. My short time on the Faculty Council was praised by its chair **[Faculty Council Member Evaluation]**. I've also served the college in ad hoc forms. I volunteered to lead the Fall 2025 efforts to revise the Faculty Council charter after faculty voted to temporarily suspend the council. These efforts led to a successful restart of Faculty Council in January 2026. In 2024, I served as an inter-department member to hire a tenured Department Chair for the Department of Human Factors and Social Science. And from August 2023 until July 2026 I created and maintained a Faculty Activity Reporting system on behalf of the college. This system allowed for ease of faculty submitting their activity to a single location and automated, formatted reporting of faculty activity for efficient monthly and bi-yearly reporting. I met with representatives of both College of Aviation (Kimberly Luthi) and College of Business (Maria Petrescu) to discuss how they could implement a similar system. Maria thought the set up was excellent, stating in private email correspondence: 

> I love this! Your survey is so much better than the Forms we've been using so far. If you don't mind exporting the survey so I can import it into my Qualtrics, as well as your Python script, that would be great. Our senior associate dean who's been handling this left, and I'm trying to catch up on this and make it more efficient. 

Maria was involved in creating the new Watermark activity reporting and confirmed it was partially modeled after the Faculty Reporting system I shared with her. 

## Department-Level Service
At the department level, I have sustained high-level leadership since accepting the Associate Department Chair role in February 2023. As Associate Department Chair, I performed all the routine administrative duties as well as used my expertise in data science to innovate admin processes, creating significant efficiencies. For example, course monitors in the department asked for lists of instructors teaching courses they were monitors for by term and I created programming scripts to create such lists from raw Campus Solutions reports. In addition, course monitors asked for a yearly accumulation of end-of-course evaluations for courses they monitored, which I created programming scripts to provide. 

After the department chair hiring failed in January 2024, I accepted being Acting Department Chair (while still being Associate Department Chair) until another hiring cycle could be completed successfully. I stayed on as Associate Department Chair for another year to assist the newly hired chair, Tim Smith, and passed on the role in order to start as the Program Chair for the new Bachelor in Data Science in August 2025. The first year of the program saw major success and had 54 declared students by August 2026. During these years of administrative service I continually shifted the courses I was mentor for as the Department of Mathematics, Science, and Technology had heavy faculty turnover. I also chaired one tenure-track candidate search in Physical Science in 2024-2025 while participating as a member in three other candidate searches: tenured candidate in Computer/Data Science in 2026, tenured department chair in 2024 **[Higgins Search Letter]**, and tenure-track candidate in Data Science in 2022-2023. 

# Professional Society Service 
At the professional society level, I have also sustained high-level leadership in the Mathematical Association of America (MAA). From 2021 until 2022 I was a member of the MAA Subcommittee on Technologies in Mathematics Education (STME), then took over as STME chair and have led the committee since. STME regularly hosts contributed paper sessions at conferences, produces virtual programming surrounding innovative technology in mathematics education, has hosted a 2-day virtual conference on Generative AI in mathematics education, and led multiple grants in collaboration with MAA to support mathematics instructor use of Generative AI. My leadership in STME has been praised, with the chair of the Committee on Teaching in Undergraduate Mathematics (CTUM) and chair of the Council on Teaching and Learning (CTL) inviting me to chair CTUM starting June 2028 **[CTUM Chair Invitation]**. 

Beyond my formal STME involvement with MAA, I have also been informally included in MAA initiatives and events. In February 2025 I was invited to an informal meet-and-greet with a representative at the Gates Foundation as part of the MAA's work in connecting the Gates Foundation with exemplary MAA members **[Informal Gates Meet and Greet]**. I was also invited to represent the MAA to talk about Generative AI in mathematics education at a *Coalition for National Science Funding* (CNSF) that would include scheduled meetings with congress members **[CNSF Invitation]**, though the MAA later decided not to attend the event. To capture the exceptional formal and informal service I've performed for MAA, I requested and attached a letter of service from MAA's Chief Strategy and Operations Officer Audrey Malagon **[MAA Executive - Service Review Letter]**.
