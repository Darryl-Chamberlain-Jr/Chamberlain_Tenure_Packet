# Chamberlain_Tenure_Packet
 Tenure Packet for Darryl Chamberlain

Required Documents: 
- **Application Statement (4 pgs)**: Applicant must submit a statement, no more than four-pages in length, supporting the application, addressing teaching performance, scholarly activity (if required), and service (if required) over the last five (5) years or eligibility period. 
- **CV**
- **Teaching Statement (2 pgs)**: Applicant must submit a two-page Teaching Summary, supporting the applicant's teaching performance over the last five (5) years or eligibility period. *Automatically added: All student evaluations and teaching/mentoring itemized report from Watermark activity*
- **Scholarly Activity (2 pgs)**: Applicant must submit a two-page Scholarly Activity Summary, supporting the applicant's scholarly and research activity (if required) over the last five (5) years or eligibility period. *Automatically added: All scholarly activity itemized report from Watermark activity*
- **Service (2 pgs)**: Applicant must submit a two-page Service Summary, supporting the applicant's service performance over the last five (5) years or eligibility period. *Automatically added: All service activity itemized report from Watermark activity*

Optional Artifacts: 
- **Teaching**: Examples of Artifacts may include but are not limited to: Peer Evaluations, CTLE Observation, Curriculum Innovation, etc.
- **Scholarship**: Examples of Artifacts may include but are not limited to: publications, presentations, grants, research, etc.
- **Service**: Examples of Artifacts may include but are not limited to: appointment letters, letters from committee chairs, etc.
